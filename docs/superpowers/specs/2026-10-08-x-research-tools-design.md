# X Research Tools — Hezuo

## Status: retired

This design is obsolete and must not be used as an implementation or deployment guide for Hezuo. The former provider-specific integration and relay instructions have been removed.

The proposed read-only tools were `x_search`, `x_user_posts`, `x_post`, and `x_profile`. This document does not establish that they are currently available or included in any Hezuo plan.

A replacement design must identify an approved provider, verified service endpoints, credential ownership, authorization, quotas, and billing before implementation. No replacement endpoint, credential variable, header, or paid-plan entitlement is specified here. Do not infer any of those from this retired document.
