# A server of the person's own as their bots' home

## Retired scope

The upstream own-home promotion, move/freeze and cross-place discovery flows
and their dedicated fixtures and tests are retired. There is no current recipe
for moving bots into a personal Cloud or discovering bots across saved places,
and no equivalent replacement is claimed. Retained desktop connections and
local computer capabilities do not establish those flows.

## Preserved safety boundaries

Historical demonstrations used synthetic places, signed fixture services and
fake engines. They were not real migration, hosted-service or deployment
qualification. Never use the user's live home, credentials or installed app as
an isolated fixture, and do not restore the removed production mode to satisfy
old tests.

A server shared with other members must not be promoted as a private home or
receive private cross-place disclosures. Cross-place hints must not reveal
addresses, IDs, messages or team-only information. Suggestions require explicit
user choice; they must not silently move bots, replace an existing home or sign
out another place. Lending computer access must not silently enable terminal
access or carry on after the server becomes shared. Welcome/tour pages and
remote pages must not gain a privileged desktop bridge merely to display a
suggestion. These are retained safety requirements, not current feature claims.
